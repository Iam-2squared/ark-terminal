# Phase57 LONG-only Entry research: FAST-FAIL policy

Effective 2026-09-17, from MSH-Entry LONG v2.2 onward. This policy does not
retroactively change the v2 or v2.1 contracts, results, or verdicts.

Hypothesis → minimal preregistered protocol → bounded Development evidence →
early-kill decision → full Development only for a surviving hypothesis.

The goal is a sufficiently strong frozen Entry and an integrated LONG-only
pipeline, not indefinite Entry optimization. Reuse Historical / Development /
outcome-exposed evidence. Never describe this reuse as Fresh, OOS, or prospective.

## Kill weak hypotheses promptly

Stop when the precommitted screen shows unstable chronological or cross-symbol
direction, winner destruction needed for risk reduction, negligible throughput,
dependence on one or two symbols, a target-role mismatch, or unresolved comparison
coverage that cannot be repaired within the bounded evidence task.

Do not rescue a failed screen through threshold interpolation, new features,
model complexity, relaxed gates, removed folds, special symbol rules, or Fresh
data. A materially different architecture requires its own decision and contract.
D30 threshold veto has now failed both replacement and v1-refinement experiments;
do not extend it by changing 2 to 1.5 or 2.5.

Use few targets chosen for their economic meaning before measuring them. Each
input needs a causal mechanism, availability and missing-data semantics. Start
with one low-capacity model. Nonlinear models require reproducible residual
structure, sample support, and cross-symbol evidence first.

## Decision and budget

- `FAST_FAIL_CONTINUE`: all preregistered structural-screen requirements pass;
  worth a separate full Development contract. This is not a candidate PASS.
- `FAST_FAIL_KILL`: an observable required condition fails. Save the result,
  propose one next architecture, and stop without implementing that alternative.
- `FAST_FAIL_INCONCLUSIVE`: genuinely insufficient or invalid evidence. At most
  one cheap resolution using saved data; if still unknown, deprioritize and stop.
  A failed performance requirement is not INCONCLUSIVE.

The v2.2 first screen allows one target, one feature set, one Ridge setting,
four chronological fits and five held-symbol fits on the last evaluation window.
No threshold search, extra target trial, or unplanned model fit is authorized by
that screen. Synthetic verification fits are separate and never use Project data.

## Invariants

Keep Frozen Selector/v1 baseline/selected LONG EXIT/Equal/cash ledger unchanged.
Keep PIT boundaries, train-only preprocessing, purged chronological groups,
symbol-disjoint checks, no future inputs, reproducibility, tests and CI.
Portfolio unresolved exposure is separate from Entry signal quality: retain
UNKNOWN equity/DD, never invent fills or cash release. Portfolio is secondary
until coverage is fair. Preserve the Fresh195 budget and its frozen allocation.

No Fresh/OOS/Prospective or new price-provider requests. LONG-only cash equity;
SHORT, margin, leverage and all nine execution/promotion permissions remain zero
or false. Do not merge main. Freeze a candidate and its evaluation contract before
any separate instruction to use Fresh data.
