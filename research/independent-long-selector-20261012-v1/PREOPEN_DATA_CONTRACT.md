- Main labels use same-security/session/market RAW O/H/L/C as exact decimal
  strings. All-null official OHLCV is UNKNOWN; partial null, NaN, Infinity,
  boolean prices, H<O and negative quantities are errors. No 09:00 timestamp
  is invented. Missing minute paths do not erase valid official daily labels.
- Comparable RAW history resets at missing sessions, Null bars and action
  boundaries that cannot be reconstructed causally. No future-restated AdjC
  or price filling. A short history makes long indicators UNKNOWN.

  The delayed file availability is checked separately against next cutoff.
  Same-time, same-price, same-quantity prints are preserved by source ordinal.
  No true aggressor, quotes, order additions or cancellations are inferred.
- Finance revisions use disclosure date/time; valuation Date alone is not an
  availability certificate. Units and publication evidence must be bound.
  Historical publication schedules are explicit assumptions with no claim of
  actual delivery or the original historical version. Observed, published,
  effective, available and captured timestamps remain distinct.
- Daily/weekly/monthly EMA is SMA-seeded. RSI/ATR/ADX use explicit Wilder
  smoothing. Ichimoku spans are computed at as-of and are not the displayed
  shifted cloud. MACD traces are linear and do not re-evaluate every prefix.
- Prior market/sector breadth and ranks use only prior prices of the as-of
  known eligible peer universe. Action-ambiguous horizons remain missing.
  Public forecasts can be facts; future realized results cannot.
- State/Dictionary is not natively joined. Prior State needs its immutable
  artifact/contract and causal upstream fit lineage. SourceId alone is insufficient.

## Private stage order

1. Metadata-only session reconciliation and current use basis.
2. One original stream into SQLite, exact source checkpoint and access receipt.
   Non-admitted rows are lexically traversed and counted; their numeric prices
   are not parsed or emitted. Original gzip is never recompressed as an original.
3. Per-row calculations into float64 columnar shards with shared history in
   SQLite. Long price history is not duplicated into every persisted snapshot.
4. Fit/calibration only, model/hash/schema frozen, then all Report watchlists.
5. Report source descriptor is read and outcome prices parsed, then evaluation.
   The terminal Report day's prices are not numerically admitted as features.
   Earlier Report prices may be valid D-1 history for a later Report snapshot;
   this exposure is recorded and is not called wholly unopened.

HGB config/head budget, cutoff08:50, cap15 and prior-price>75 remain fixed.
Historical source coverage and live refresh are separate gates. Legal reuse,
protected-session, source hash, class support and memory gates remain enforced.
The old intraday price75 policy uses a decision price; the preopen prototype
uses prior RAW close. They are not identical eligibility policies.

Native-scale RAM/disk/runtime and all-family live supply are NOT certified.
No actual market feature row, fit, calibration, watchlist or evaluation was
generated by this work. See the access blockers and private checkpoint.
