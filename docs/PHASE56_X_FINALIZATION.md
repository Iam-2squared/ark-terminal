# Phase56.x Chart Intelligence Finalization

Phase56 closes only the chart-understanding research layer. It does not authorize recommendations, paper trading, broker writes, or live trading.

The final gate verifies that chart structure, price action, setup context, entry research, entry OOS evaluation, classic-pattern context, pattern OOS evidence, and unnamed-structure discovery have all been evaluated. At least one OOS evidence candidate is required before Phase56 can become a research review candidate.

A pass yields `PHASE56_REVIEW_CANDIDATE` only. Execution, broker/Excel/RSS writes, paper/live trading, automatic promotion, and production updates remain disabled.
