NOW is a deterministic causal descriptor, explicitly NOT Ground Truth. Direction is UP/DOWN/UNCHANGED over the latest five scheduled closed trading minutes. Structure is UP_STRUCTURE/DOWN_STRUCTURE/RANGE_STRUCTURE/NONE. Phase is the possibly-multilabel set PROGRESSION/CORRECTION/RECOVERY/BALANCE/RESTRUCTURING. DEFINED/NONE, DEFINED/empty Phase, INSUFFICIENT and NOT_EVALUATED remain distinct.

The later recognition targets are future_resolution_v2.adjudicatedStructureAtT and adjudicatedPhaseAtT: delayed-confirmation adjudication of the state AT t within fixed H=10 active minutes. They are NOT the state at t+10, a future-return label, or copying the already-causal NOW Direction. PROGRESSION can follow either an up or down structure; RECOVERY does not necessarily imply UP_STRUCTURE.
